How it plays

Six rules, all of them on screen: a viewer gets the game from its first second.

  1. Two balls, one pitchEach a club in its shirt, or a player in his country's, flying at one speed and bouncing off the ring and off each other.
  2. The goal spinsIts mouth turns round the ring all match, and the other way from half time, as the sides change ends.
  3. Through it is a goalA ball that flies through the mouth scores. One that clips a post bounces off it: POST!
  4. Kick-off againAfter each goal both balls go back to the centre spot and away. The clock runs on, as a referee's does.
  5. Ninety minutesThe clock fills the timeline over your video. Level at 90', the next goal wins: golden goal.
  6. A crowd for each sideThe home side's fans on the left, the away side's on the right, each half on its feet when its side scores.

What you choose

Everything on one card at the top of the studio. Leave any of it to the dice.

  • Clubs or players

    157 clubs: the Champions League, Europe's five big leagues, the Balkans, South America and beyond. Or 453 players, today's stars and the legends.

  • Found as you type

    A few letters are enough: rma, zvezda, barsa, cr7. Nicknames, short names and spellings without accents all find their side.

  • 201 classic matches

    The derbies, Europe's great nights, the GOAT debate, today's rivals, then and now. Beside each, how many times you made it and who won.

  • The winner

    Pick the side that wins, or let the match decide. Four matches in five end on a comeback or a late winner.

  • The length

    From 20 to 90 seconds. Ninety minutes always fit it: three to six goals in 30 seconds, five to ten in a minute.

  • Whistles and a narrator

    A whistle at each kick-off, the crowd's roar on a goal, three at full time. A narrator names both sides and calls the winner (paid plans).

Questions

About Goal Rush. The rest are on the home page.

Is the result random?

It is worked out before it plays, from a seed, and balanced: the studio tries seeds until the match ends on your length, is not a draw, has its goals spread through it and none in the first seconds. The same settings always play the same match.

Can I choose who wins?

Yes. Pick the winner on the match card and the studio finds a match that side wins, comeback and all. Leave it open and either side can.

Does it show club crests or player photos?

No. Clubs and players are drawn as balls in their colours: a club's shirt with its letters, a player's national shirt and number. The names only say who plays; BounceReel is not affiliated with any club, league or player.

How many goals are there?

Three to six in a 30-second video, five to ten in a minute: about one every five seconds, the first after the opening seconds and none of them long apart.
