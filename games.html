Ball Race

Every plan

Two balls race through layers of ever bigger numbers: Fibonacci against factorial, primes against squares.

How it plays

  1. Every floor hit deals a numberEach ball hits with the next number of its sequence: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5… against 1, 2, 6, 24, 120…
  2. Break every layerA layer breaks once its number has been dealt, and the first ball through its last one reaches the line.
  3. Close to the very endSpeeds are set so it ends close, a photo finish or a gap of 1 to 10 s that you pick, at the length you pick.

  • 79pairings for the dice
  • 3looks
  • 4powers

What you choose

  • Two sequences, or a roll of the dice
  • A photo finish or a gap
  • Paper, night or cards
  • A power: speeds up, slows down, grows or splits
  • A crowd of balls against one

Arena Clash

Paid plans

Flags in one round arena, each tied to the ring by a fan of strings. Cut theirs, keep yours: the last flag standing wins.

How it plays

  1. Every bounce ties ten stringsOff the ring, where the ball hit it, however many it already holds.
  2. A touch cutsA ball that touches a rival's strings cuts every one it touches, at once. Its own it passes through.
  3. The last flag standing winsA flag with no strings left is out. The balls speed up as it goes, and the last outs play in slow motion.
  • 199flags
  • 2–12flags a battle
  • 6regions for the dice
  • 4 in 5end on a comeback

What you choose

  • Every flag, or a roll of the dice
  • A region: Europe, the Balkans, the Americas, Asia, Africa or Oceania
  • The winner, or let the arena decide
  • From 2 flags to 12, made for 3 or 4

In both games

Whichever game you pick, the video around it is made the same way, and comes out ready to post.

  • The length you pick

    From 20 to 90 seconds, each game balanced to end on it, to a tenth of a second.

  • A narrator

    It opens the video with a question for the comments and calls the winner at the end. On the paid plans; every plan records music only or silence.

  • A follow button

    Once while the game plays and again under the winner card, tapped by a hand. On the paid plans.

  • A loop with no cut

    The last half second fades into the first frame, so the replay has no seam.

  • Your library

    Every recording with its settings and links. Open one in the studio and it plays out again, frame for frame.

  • Ready to post

    An MP4 at 1080×1920 and 60 fps, laid out around the apps' buttons, for Shorts, TikTok and Reels as it is.
