Two games, one studio

Every BounceReel video is a game played out on screen and balanced to end at the length you pick. Pick the game at the top of the studio: the length, the sound, the narrator, recording and your library work the same in both.

  • Ball Race

    Every plan

    Two balls race through layers of ever bigger numbers: Fibonacci against factorial, primes against squares.

    • Each ball hits the floor with the numbers of its sequence, and the first to break every layer reaches the line.
    • Balanced to end close at the length you pick, down to a photo finish.
    • A new matchup every roll of the dice: fourteen sequences, 65 pairings, and powers for a twist on the paid plans.
    • Three looks: paper, night and cards.

  • Arena Clash

    Paid plans

    Flags in one round arena, each tied to the ring by a fan of strings. Cut theirs, keep yours: the last flag standing wins.

    • Every bounce off the ring ties ten new strings, and a flag that touches a rival's strings cuts them.
    • A flag with no strings left is out. The balls speed up as it goes, and the last outs play in slow motion.
    • Made for three or four flags, from 199 countries; pick the winner yourself, or let the arena decide.
    • Balanced to end at the length you pick, on a black stage made for the feed.
