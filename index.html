    Which browsers does the studio run in?

    Chrome or Edge on a computer: they record the MP4 (H.264 video, AAC sound) that Shorts, TikTok and Reels take as it is. Firefox records WebM, which TikTok and Instagram want converted first. Safari is not tested yet. The studio shows which format your browser writes, beside Record.

    Can I make videos on my phone?

    Not yet: the studio needs a computer. The videos are made for phones, though: vertical and full size, ready to post from wherever you post.

    Do I have to edit the video?

    No. Each recording comes out at the length you set, with the race, the winner card and the fade back to the first frame. Upload the file as it is.

    How close are the races?

    As close as you ask. A photo finish puts the loser 0.15 to 1.1 seconds behind the winner; or pick a gap of 1 to 10 seconds. Either way the speeds are set so the video comes out at the length you chose.

    Is there a watermark?

    On the free plan, a small BounceReel watermark, the mark and the site's address, sits between the two cards of every video. The paid plans have none.

    Can I use my own sound?

    Yes. Record with music only or silent, then add a sound in TikTok, Instagram or YouTube when you post.

    What does the narrator cost?

    It comes with the paid plans, at no extra cost: the narrator is Kokoro, a free voice that runs in your browser, so what it says is made on your computer. The first time, your browser downloads the voice once.

    Can I hear the voices first?

    Yes. The studio's Voice tab plays each voice saying your matchup, on every plan.

    Can I make the same video again?

    Yes. Every race comes from a seed, and your library keeps each video's settings: open one in the studio and it plays out again, frame for frame.

    Where are my videos stored?

    The MP4 downloads to your computer. Your library keeps each video's settings and links, not the file: the settings are enough to make the same video again.

    What happens when my library is full?

    A recording still downloads; it is just not added to your library until you delete one there, or move to a plan that keeps more.

    How do I delete my account?

    On your account page. The account, its videos and their links are deleted at once.
