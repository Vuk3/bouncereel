    Which browsers does the studio run in?

    Chrome or Edge on a computer: they record the MP4 (H.264 video, AAC sound) that Shorts, TikTok and Reels take as it is. Firefox records WebM, which TikTok and Instagram want converted first. Safari is not tested yet, and phones and tablets cannot record yet. The studio shows which format your browser writes, beside Record.

    Do I have to edit the video?

    No. Each recording comes out at the length you set, with the game, the winner card and the fade back to the first frame. Upload the file as it is.

    How close are the races?

    As close as you ask. A photo finish puts the loser 0.15 to 1.1 seconds behind the winner; or pick a gap of 1 to 10 seconds. Either way the speeds are set so the video comes out at the length you chose.

    Can I choose who wins an Arena Clash?

    Yes: pick the winner in the battle's card, or leave it to the arena. Either way the battle is balanced to end at the length you set, with its flags going out along the way rather than all at once.

    Is there a watermark?

    On the free plan, a small BounceReel watermark, the mark and the site's address: between the two cards of a race, under the arena of a battle. The paid plans have none.

    What does the narrator cost?

    It comes with the paid plans, at no extra cost: the narrator is Kokoro, a free voice that runs in your browser, so what it says is made on your computer. The first time, your browser downloads the voice once. Or record with music only or silent, and add a sound when you post.

    Can I make the same video again?

    Yes. Every game comes from a seed, and your library keeps each video's settings: open one in the studio and it plays out again, frame for frame.

    Where are my videos stored?

    The MP4 downloads to your computer. Your library keeps each video's settings and links, not the file: the settings are enough to make the same video again.
