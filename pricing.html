    What is the difference between Pro and Champion?

    Pro makes all three games, without the watermark: five videos a day up to a minute, with music or silent. Champion makes everything: no daily limit, videos up to 90 seconds, the narrator, the follow button, the winner you pick, your own words on screen and in the narrator's mouth, and every new game the day it comes out.

    Is paying by the year cheaper?

    Yes: Pro is $7 a month paid by the year ($84) instead of $9 a month, and Champion $15 a month ($180) instead of $19. Over 20% off either way.

    What happens to my videos if I change plans?

    They stay in your library. On a plan that keeps fewer videos, nothing is deleted: new recordings are just not added until there is room.
