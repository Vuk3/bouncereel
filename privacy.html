3. What stays on your computer

  • The videos themselves. The studio records each MP4 in your browser and saves it to your computer. We never receive it.
  • The studio's settings, in your browser's own storage: your current matchup, the light or dark theme, the powers switch, whether you have seen the studio's first hint, and any recording waiting to be added to your library while you are offline. Signing out clears your account's settings from that browser.
  • The narrator. The voice, Kokoro, runs in your browser. The first time you use the narrator or hear a voice, your browser downloads the voice model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees your IP address as any website you visit does. What the narrator says is made on your computer and is not sent anywhere.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account, plan and library: as long as you have an account. Deleting your account on your account page deletes your email address, password hash, sessions, plan, and every video and link at once.
  • The record of changes stays after an account is deleted, as a history of what changed, with only the account's number: not its email address.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, and 30 days at most.
  • One-time links: confirming your address works for 7 days, setting a new password for 1 hour; each is deleted once it has expired.
  • Counts of attempts (security): about a day; they are cleared as new attempts come in.
  • Technical logs: a few days, and at most 7.
  • Backups: our database keeps a rolling history of up to 30 days for recovery, so data you delete is gone from it within 30 days.
  • Mails: Resend keeps a record of the mails it sends for a limited time, under its own retention policy.
  • Messages to us: as long as we need them to answer you, and no longer than two years.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;

  • restrict how we use it, or object to a use based on our legitimate interest;

Much of this you can do yourself on your account page and the Videos page. For anything else, write to %CONTACT_EMAIL% from your account's email address, so we know the request is yours. We answer within one month; a complicated request can take up to two months more, and we will tell you if it does. It costs nothing.

In Serbia, the authority is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Poverenik za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti), Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11120 Belgrade, poverenik.rs. In the EU or the UK, you can also complain to the authority where you live or work. We would like to hear from you first, though: most things are quicker to sort out by email.

10. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, sign-in sessions and one-time links as hashes of random tokens, and the session cookie cannot be read by scripts. Sign-in, sign-up and password-reset attempts are limited, each account can read only its own library, and the admin area is open to the operator alone. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you without undue delay, and the authority within 72 hours where the law requires it.

11. Children

BounceReel is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 16 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.
